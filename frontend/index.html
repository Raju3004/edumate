EduMate reads what a learner actually understands — not just which grade they're in — and adapts the explanation, the pace, and the follow-up questions in real time.

01 — The Problem

Every classroom is taught to an average student who doesn't exist.

One instructor, thirty or forty learners, one fixed pace. The learner who's ahead disengages. The one who's behind stays quiet rather than ask again. Neither gets what they actually need — and no instructor can be in forty places at once.

1instructor in the room
40different paces in that room
1tutor that adapts to each one
02 — The Fix

"One tutor. Wholly adapted to you."

EduMate doesn't hand every learner the same explanation. It builds a working model of what one specific person understands, then teaches to that — adjusting depth, examples, and pace as it learns more.

03 — How It Works

Two things, done properly.

Learning Profile

EduMate figures out what a learner actually understands — not just what they got right.

  • Reads for understanding, not just correct answers
  • Distinguishes a real explanation from a lucky guess
  • Tracks pace and gaps over time, not just scores

Tutor Relationship

The tutor a learner can actually push back on.

  • "I don't understand" is a normal thing to say
  • Re-explains differently, not just louder
  • Encourages instead of grading every exchange
